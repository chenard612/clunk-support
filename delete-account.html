Delete your Clunk account

You can permanently delete your Clunk account and its data at any time. You do not need the app installed.

In the app

Go to Account → Delete Account and confirm by typing your username. Deletion runs immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email alexis.chenard@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Clunk account” and your username. We will confirm that you own the account and complete the deletion within 30 days. This also works for accounts created with Google or Facebook sign-in.

What deletion removes

  • Your profile: name, username, email, address and profile picture.
  • Your garage and every car in it.
  • Every maintenance record, with its photos, videos and voice notes.
  • Quotes you submitted as a mechanic, and your notifications.

Maintenance you recorded on someone else's car stays with that car, with your name removed, so the vehicle's owner does not lose their own history. De-identified, aggregated vehicle data that can no longer be tied to you may be kept. Backups are overwritten on a rolling 30-day cycle, after which no copy remains.
