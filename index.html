Clunk Support

Clunk keeps your cars, their service history and your receipts in one place, and uses that record to connect you with mechanics, parts and maintenance information.

Contact us

For help with the app, a bug report, or a question about your account, email alexis.chenard@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I add a car?

Open the Garage tab, tap the add button and enter the make, model and year. A VIN is optional.

How do I record maintenance?

Open a car from your garage and add a maintenance record. You can attach photos, videos, voice notes and written notes.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address on your account and we will help you regain access.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete Account. You can also request deletion without the app.
