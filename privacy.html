Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Clunk is a vehicle-records platform. You keep your cars, your service history and your receipts in one place, and we use that record to connect you with mechanics, parts and maintenance information. This page explains what we collect, why we hold it, who else sees it, where it is stored, how long we keep it, and how to get it deleted.

1. Who we are

Clunk ("Clunk", "we", "us") operates the Clunk mobile applications and their companion website. We are the party responsible for the personal information described here. For any privacy question or request, write to alexis.chenard@gmail.com.

Clunk is operated from Canada. The data we hold is processed and stored on Microsoft Azure infrastructure in the United States — see section 6.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the product needs to work. In practice that is:

Account information

  • Your name, username and email address.
  • A password, stored only as a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash. We never store or have access to your password in readable form.
  • Your profile picture, if you upload one.
  • Your address, if you provide one, plus the approximate latitude and longitude we derive from it. We use it to match you with mechanics and services near you. You can leave it blank.
  • The date your account was created, and whether it is a driver or mechanic account.

Vehicle and maintenance records

  • Each car you add: make, model, trim, year, colour, purchase date, and the VIN if you enter one.
  • Odometer readings, service history, oil, tire and brake change records, tire and wheel fitment details, and condition or wear notes.
  • Anything you attach to a maintenance record — photos, videos, voice notes and written notes.
  • Listing details if you mark a car or part for sale, or make a car publicly visible.

Marketplace activity

  • Service requests you post, quotes and bids exchanged with mechanics, messages sent through the platform, and the notifications we generate for you.

Technical information

  • Server logs containing your IP address, request times, the endpoints you called and error details. These exist for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
  • Basic device and browser information sent by your client, and a sign-in token we store in your browser's local storage (or the app's secure storage) to keep you signed in.

We do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers, and we do not use cookies for advertising.

If you sign in with Google or Facebook

  • We receive your name, email address, the provider's account identifier and your profile picture URL. We do not receive your password, and we do not post anything to those accounts.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your garage — store and show your cars, their specifications, their history and their media.
  • To connect you with service — surface maintenance that is due, generate service opportunities, and show them to mechanics within a reasonable distance of the address you gave us.
  • To produce maintenance information — estimate wear, flag upcoming service, and match parts and tire fitments to your specific vehicle.
  • To improve the platform — understand which features are used and where things break, working from aggregated data wherever that is sufficient.
  • To keep accounts safe — authentication, rate limiting, and investigating abuse or fraud.
  • To meet legal obligations — respond to lawful requests and keep records we are required to keep.

Where the law requires consent, we rely on the consent you give by creating an account and entering this data. Otherwise we rely on our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service.

4. We keep the vehicle record

This is the part that is specific to Clunk, so it is stated plainly: we aggregate and retain the vehicle data entered on the platform. Concretely:

  • A car's history outlives your ownership of it. When you remove a car from your garage, the car record and its maintenance history are not destroyed. They are detached from your garage and kept, so that a later owner of the same vehicle can pick the service history back up.
  • Service performed by others stays with the car. If a mechanic records work on your vehicle, that record remains attached to the vehicle even if their account is later deleted.
  • We build aggregate datasets. We combine vehicle, wear and service data across the platform to produce statistics and maintenance insights — typical service intervals, common failures, realistic wear rates for a given model. These datasets are de-identified: they describe vehicles and repairs, not named people.
  • We may share and publish aggregate results, and use them in features and reporting. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your name, email address, address, contact details or your media as part of any aggregate dataset.

Deleting your account removes your personal information and your copy of the records (see section 7), but de-identified and aggregated vehicle data that can no longer be tied back to you may be retained indefinitely.

5. Who else sees it

We share personal information only in the cases below.

Mechanics and other users on the platform

  • When you post a service request or a car generates a maintenance opportunity, mechanics within range can see the vehicle, the work needed, the relevant history and media you attached, and your approximate location. Your contact details are shared with a mechanic through the platform when you engage with them.
  • If you mark a car or part public or for sale, the details you listed are visible to other users.

Service providers who process data for us

  • Microsoft Azure — application hosting, the PostgreSQL database, and Blob Storage for photos, videos and voice notes. United States.
  • Google and Meta (Facebook) — only if you choose to sign in with them, to verify your identity at sign-in.
  • AI processing providers — when the optional AI documentation feature is enabled for your account, the photos and notes on the specific maintenance record you are documenting are sent to a third-party model provider (currently OpenRouter) to produce a written summary. Images are downscaled before sending. Nothing is sent unless you trigger the feature on a record.

Public vehicle data sources

To fill in specifications, fuel economy and parts information we query outside services including the U.S. NHTSA vPIC database, fueleconomy.gov, vehicle specification APIs and parts marketplaces. These queries carry vehicle identifiers — make, model, year, and where needed a VIN — and never your name, email address or contact details.

Legal and corporate

We disclose information where we are legally required to, and to protect our rights or someone's safety. If the business is sold or reorganised, data may transfer as part of that transaction; this policy, or one at least as protective, continues to apply.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Where it is processed

The application, the database and all uploaded media are hosted on Microsoft Azure in the United States (East US region). Some of the vehicle data sources and the AI provider described above also operate in the United States.

If you are located in Canada, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or elsewhere outside the United States, your information is transferred to and stored in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under the laws of that country. We rely on contractual protections with our providers for these transfers, and by using Clunk you acknowledge this transfer.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account information — kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your profile, your garage, the cars in it, their maintenance records, and every photo, video and voice note you uploaded, including from blob storage.
  • Media you delete yourself — removed from storage when the record it belongs to is deleted.
  • Server logs — retained for a short operational period for security and debugging, then discarded.
  • Vehicle records no longer linked to you — retained as described in section 4, without your identifying information attached.
  • De-identified and aggregated data — may be kept indefinitely.
  • We may keep limited records longer where we must to comply with a legal obligation or to resolve a dispute.

8. Your choices and your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and correct your information — from Account Settings in the app.
  • Delete your account and data — see section 9.
  • Remove individual cars, records or media — from the car's maintenance history.
  • Leave out optional data — an address, a VIN or a profile picture are not required to use the product.
  • Get a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, object to a particular use, or withdraw consent, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a privacy regulator — in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial equivalent. We ask that you contact us first so we can try to resolve it.

9. How to request deletion

There are two ways, and both permanently delete the same data:

  • In the app — Account Settings → Delete Account. You confirm by typing your username. This runs immediately and cannot be undone.
  • By email — write to alexis.chenard@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will verify that you own the account and complete the deletion within 30 days. This works after you have uninstalled the app. See Delete your account.

Deletion removes your profile, your garage, your cars, their maintenance records and all media you uploaded. Service records you created on other people's vehicles remain with those vehicles with your identity removed, so that other owners do not lose their history, and de-identified aggregate data is retained as described in section 4.

10. Security

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashes. Traffic to the service is encrypted in transit with TLS. Sessions use signed, expiring tokens. Uploaded media is held in a private storage container with no public URLs and is served only through authenticated endpoints. Sign-in attempts are rate limited.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate regulator where the law requires it.

11. Children

Clunk is not intended for children. Do not create an account if you are under 16. If we learn that we hold information about a child under 16, we delete it. A parent or guardian can contact us at alexis.chenard@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top will change with it. If a change materially affects how we use your information we will give notice in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use Clunk after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact us

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: alexis.chenard@gmail.com. We answer privacy requests within 30 days.
